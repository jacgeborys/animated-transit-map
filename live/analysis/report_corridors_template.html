Warsaw tram corridors: which way, which street, which hour

The first report found where trams stand still. This one splits every signalised junction into its movements (from which arm to which arm), ranks the street corridors by how fast trams really get along them, and shows how the slow spots spread through the day. Same data: every tram GPS fix of one Tuesday tied to its scheduled trip.

Companion to Where Warsaw's trams wait.

Where would priority pay off most? Capacity against speed

Each dot is one corridor in one direction. Further right = more places per hour in the morning peak (07–09), lower = slower, bigger = more passenger-hours a day spent standing away from platforms (load-factor assumption: 50 % peak / 25 % off-peak). The lower right corner is where priority and better signal plans serve the most people.
